Opportunities

Opportunities

If you are interested in playing with bacteria in order to understand how biological mechanisms determine evolution, get in touch – irrespective of whether you are a wet or a dry scientist.

Enquiries welcome

Postdoc

Funded positions are advertised when they exist. We are also happy to put together fellowship applications with prospective postdocs.

Enquiries welcome

PhD

PhD places generally run through university and doctoral training programme competitions, which have fixed deadlines. Get in touch well before one to talk through a project.

Enquiries welcome

Master's projects

Master’s projects run every year. Several Master’s students in the group have gone on to do a PhD here.

Enquiries welcome

Internships & visits

Internships and short research visits are possible. Say what you would want to work on and what funding, if any, you have.

What to put in the email

A short, specific email is more useful than a long general one. Four things are enough:

  • What you are looking for, and roughly when you would want to start.
  • Which of the group's work you found interesting, and what you would want to ask next.
  • What you have done before — techniques, coding, any project write-up you are proud of.
  • A CV attached. One or two pages is plenty.
